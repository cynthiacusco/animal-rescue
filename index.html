Our Mission

We are a small group of local volunteers dedicated to helping pets in our neighborhood.
Our goal is simple: to provide temporary medical care, find warm foster homes, and safely place these animals with loving families who will cherish them forever.

A Message From Our Coordinator

Our Impact

Because we are a non profit ornanization and we rely on community donations, every single success matters. Here is what we could accomplish together last year:

  • 84 Local pets successfully treated and adopted into forever families.
  • 22 Kind foster households who opened their doors so animals could stay out of cages.
  • 100% Every donated dollar went straight to food and vet bills.

How You Can Help

We cannot do this alone. Since we don't have a shelter building, we completely rely on people like you. Whether you have space in your home to foster a pet for a few weeks,
time to help us clean kennels on weekends, or room in your heart to adopt, your choice directly saves a neighborhood life.
